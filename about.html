About Samrat Electricals

A new name in Korauta with an old-fashioned promise — honest advice, genuine products and service with a smile.

Our story

Bringing the big-showroom experience to our neighbourhood

Samrat Electricals (सम्राट इलेक्ट्रिकल्स) has newly opened at Gopalpur, Korauta, Varanasi to serve families, shops and builders in our area with everything electric — from LED bulbs and switches to smart TVs, refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners.

For too long, people here had to travel into the city to compare models or find genuine products. We wanted to change that. Our shop brings a wide range of trusted brands close to home, with fair prices, proper bills and the kind of personal service only a local shop can offer.

Whether you are setting up a new home, upgrading an old appliance or just need a fan before summer — we're here to help you choose right.
